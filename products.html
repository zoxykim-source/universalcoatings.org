Primer

Primer

A surface preparation coating designed to improve paint adhesion, seal porous surfaces, and enhance the durability and finish of subsequent paint coats. Helps create a uniform base for topcoats and improves overall paint performance.

Composition

  • Quality binders, sealing agents, pigments and performance additives for strong adhesion and surface sealing

How to Apply

  • Clean the surface, remove dust/grease/loose material
  • Stir well before use
  • Apply 1 coat evenly by brush, roller or spray
  • Allow to dry before topcoat

Applications

  • Interior and exterior walls
  • Concrete, plaster, brick and masonry
  • New and previously painted surfaces

Storage

  • Store in a cool, dry place
  • Keep container tightly closed
Italian Stucco

Italian Stucco

A decorative wall finish that creates a smooth, marble-like appearance for elegant interior spaces. Delivers a luxurious, polished look while enhancing the beauty and durability of walls.

Composition

  • High-quality acrylic binders, fine mineral fillers, pigments and special additives for a glossy decorative finish

How to Apply

  • Ensure surface is smooth, clean and primed
  • Apply thin coats using a stainless steel trowel or spatula
  • Allow each coat to dry before the next
  • Burnish / polish final coat for desired sheen

Applications

  • Interior feature walls
  • Living rooms, bedrooms, offices, hotels
  • Plastered and well-prepared surfaces

Storage

  • Store cool and dry, container tightly closed
  • Protect from direct sunlight and freezing
Marbletex

Marbletex

A textured decorative wall coating designed to create a durable, stone-like finish on interior and exterior surfaces. Enhances wall appearance while adding protection against weathering and surface wear.

Composition

  • Acrylic binders, marble granules / mineral fillers, pigments and performance additives for a tough, long-lasting textured finish

How to Apply

  • Ensure surface is clean, dry and properly primed
  • Stir well before use
  • Apply with trowel, roller or textured tool
  • Allow to dry fully before exposure to moisture

Applications

  • Interior and exterior walls
  • Feature walls and boundary walls
  • Concrete, plaster and masonry surfaces

Storage

  • Cool, dry place; tightly closed
  • Protect from sunlight and freezing
Texture Paste

Texture Paste

A decorative wall coating used to create stylish textured finishes on interior and exterior surfaces. Adds depth and character to walls while providing a durable, long-lasting surface.

Composition

  • Quality acrylic binders, mineral fillers and performance additives for easy texturing and strong adhesion

How to Apply

  • Ensure surface is clean, dry and primed
  • Stir well before use
  • Apply with trowel, spatula, roller or textured tools
  • Allow to dry before painting or sealing if required

Applications

  • Interior and exterior feature walls
  • Homes, offices, shops, commercial spaces
  • Concrete, plaster and masonry surfaces

Storage

  • Cool, dry place; tightly closed
  • Protect from sunlight and freezing
Under Coat

Under Coat

A preparatory coating applied before finishing paints to improve coverage, enhance colour uniformity, and provide a smooth, even base for topcoats — for a better final finish and longer-lasting paintwork.

Composition

  • Quality binders, pigments, extenders and performance additives for good adhesion and strong hiding power

How to Apply

  • Clean and prepare surface; ensure dry and free of dust/grease
  • Stir well before use
  • Apply 1 even coat by brush, roller or spray
  • Allow to dry fully before finishing paint

Applications

  • Interior and exterior walls
  • Wood and metal (when compatible with topcoat)
  • Plastered, concrete and previously painted surfaces

Storage

  • Cool, dry place; tightly closed
  • Protect from sunlight and freezing

DampCoat

DampCoat

A waterproof protective coating designed to prevent dampness, moisture penetration and water seepage on walls and masonry surfaces. Forms a strong barrier against mould growth, water stains and surface damage — for interior and exterior use.

Composition

  • High-quality water-based acrylic polymers, waterproofing additives, pigments and performance-enhancing fillers

How to Apply

  • Clean surface, remove dust/loose paint/grease
  • Stir well before use
  • Apply 1–2 coats by brush, roller or spray
  • Allow each coat to dry before the next

Applications

  • Interior and exterior walls, basements and foundations
  • Bathrooms and kitchens
  • Concrete, brick and plastered surfaces

Storage

  • Cool, dry place; tightly closed
  • Protect from heat, sunlight and freezing

Budget Emulsion

Acrylic Sealant

Acrylic Sealant

A flexible, water-based sealing compound designed for filling gaps and joints. Adheres well to most building materials and provides a smooth, paintable finish once cured.

Composition

  • Acrylate polymers (butyl acrylate, ethyl acrylate)
  • Plasticizers for flexibility
  • Mineral fillers, stabilizers, VOC-controlled additives

How to Apply

  • Cut nozzle at an angle, apply with a sealant gun into clean, dry joints
  • Smooth with a spatula or damp cloth
  • Allow curing for 24 hours before painting

Applications

  • Sealing joints around doors, windows, skirting boards
  • Filling cracks in plaster, brick and concrete
  • Ideal for areas requiring painting after sealing

Storage

  • Cool, dry place; protect from frost and sunlight
  • Shelf life: 12–18 months unopened
Vinyl Matt

Vinyl Matt

A high-quality, water-based emulsion paint delivering a smooth, non-reflective finish. Formulated with durable vinyl resins for excellent coverage, easy application and long-lasting performance.

Composition

  • Lightfast pigments; acrylic copolymer emulsion binder
  • Water solvent; flow, levelling and durability additives

How to Apply

  • Stir well, apply by brush / roller / spray
  • Two coats recommended, 2–4 hrs drying between coats

Applications

  • Interior walls and ceilings
  • Large areas needing a uniform matt look
  • Ideal for modern, elegant spaces

Storage

  • Cool, dry place; protect from frost and sunlight
  • Shelf life: 12–24 months unopened
Weather Guard

Weather Guard

Premium exterior paint formulated to withstand harsh weather conditions. Provides a tough, protective coating with excellent resistance to rain, sunlight and humidity.

Composition

  • Pure acrylic copolymer emulsion for exterior durability
  • Colorfast pigments, mineral fillers
  • Fungicidal agents, UV stabilizers, hydrophobic agents

How to Apply

  • Stir well, apply by brush / roller / spray
  • Two coats recommended, 4–6 hrs drying between coats
  • Ensure surfaces are clean and dry before application

Applications

  • Exterior walls, facades and boundary walls
  • Homes, offices and commercial buildings exposed to the elements
  • Suitable for concrete, plaster, brick and cement surfaces

Storage

  • Cool, dry place; protect from frost and sunlight
  • Shelf life: 12–24 months unopened
Silk Vinyl

Silk Vinyl

A durable, water-based emulsion paint with a soft sheen finish. Combines the smooth elegance of silk with the toughness of vinyl, giving walls a subtle glow while remaining easy to clean.

  • Suitable for plaster, brick and cement surfaces

Storage
